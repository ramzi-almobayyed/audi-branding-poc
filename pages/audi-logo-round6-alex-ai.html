My recommendation

X1, Ribbon A, is the strongest of this set. Three clean strokes in navy, cyan and purple form an A that folds over itself, which suggests the parts of the flow coming together. It holds up at 16px. The Lattice A family is rich at large sizes but too detailed for a favicon. The source boards say the same thing, since they each add a simplified icon; X7 is my version of that.

About the source text

The boards use different taglines, such as "Automation Development & AI" (spelled "Automaation" in places) and "Specflow Orchestrator Dashboard". The lockups here use Augmented Unified Delivery Intelligence to match the other rounds. I can switch to any tagline you prefer.
