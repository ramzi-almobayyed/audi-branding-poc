My recommendation

P1, Pixel Tile, for the main logo. It keeps everything people liked about the Pixel A and puts it on a solid tile, so it stands out on light and dark tabs alike. For the browser favicon, use P8, Pixel-Perfect 16, or a tile version of it: its pixels land exactly on the screen grid, so it stays sharp at 16px where the original turns soft.
