Chip A, final touches

The Chip A from Round 8, with the three requested changes: pins centered on every side, a solid chip body in #0871B2, and black and white versions for dark and light backgrounds.

Selected: Chip ARound 8, M4. Updated here as C1, C2 and C3.

What changed

The dashed lines mark the center of the logo
  • Pins centeredThe three pins on each side now sit at 22, 32 and 42, so the middle pin lines up with the center of the chip on all four sides.
  • Solid bodyThe gradient is replaced by one flat color, #0871B2, behind the pixel A.
  • Black and whiteTwo grayscale versions: white chip for dark backgrounds, black chip for light backgrounds.
